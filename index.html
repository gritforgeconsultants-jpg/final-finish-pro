What We Do

Every Trade That Makes a House a Home

From bare drywall to finished floors, we handle the work that turns a structure into a space you want to live in.

Interior & Exterior Painting

Full prep, prime, and finish for homes and commercial spaces. Walls, ceilings, trim, cabinets, and exterior siding — brush, roll, or spray to spec.

Drywall Installation & Repair

Hang, tape, mud, and finish. New construction, renovation patches, water damage repair, and texture matching — Level 4 and Level 5 finishes.

Flooring Installation

Hardwood, LVP, tile, carpet, and engineered flooring. Subfloor prep, leveling, transitions, and trim — installed to manufacturer warranty standards.

Concrete Work

Patios, walkways, driveways, slabs, and repair. Finishing, stamping, staining, and overlays for residential and light commercial.

Carpentry & Trim

Crown molding, baseboards, casing, built-ins, door hanging, and finish carpentry. Custom millwork coordination for high-end finishes.

Full Remodeling

Kitchen and bathroom remodels, basement finishing, and whole-home renovation. One contractor, one point of contact, start to finish.

About Final Finish

Built on Craft.
Proven Over 25 Years.

Final Finish Pro Services started as a one-truck operation in Westminster, Colorado in 1999. What began with drywall and paint grew into a full-service finishing contractor — the kind of crew that GCs call when the work has to be right the first time.

We're still owner-operated. The person who scopes your project is the same person who stands behind the finished work. No sales reps. No hand-offs. No surprises on the invoice.

Our crews work across the Denver metro — Westminster, Arvada, Lakewood, Aurora, Boulder, and everywhere in between. Residential and commercial. We don't chase volume. We do quality work for people who can tell the difference.
